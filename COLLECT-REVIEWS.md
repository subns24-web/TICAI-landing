# Getting real reviews onto the page (fast)

The review section is placeholder-only right now. **Hide it until you have real ones** —
add `style="display:none"` to the `<section>` wrapping the reviews. An empty review
section converts better than a fake one, because sceptical buyers in this market are
actively looking for signs the page is fake.

Do not write them yourself. Published reviews that aren't from real customers are
prohibited under **BIS IS 19000:2022** and the CCPA online-review guidelines, and count
as an unfair trade practice under the Consumer Protection Act 2019. For a trainer selling
under his own name, that's not a risk worth ₹99 conversions.

## The fastest honest route

Run the first batch **free or at ₹99 for 20 people you can reach directly**. You only need
three good reviews. Collect them the same day — recall drops fast after 48 hours.

## WhatsApp message to send 2 hours after the workshop

Send individually, not to a group. Group requests get ignored; individual ones get ~40%
response.

> నమస్కారం [పేరు] గారు 🙏
>
> ఈరోజు workshop కి వచ్చినందుకు ధన్యవాదాలు.
>
> ఒక చిన్న సాయం చేస్తారా? మీ అభిప్రాయం 3–4 లైన్లలో ఇక్కడే reply చేయండి. ఈ 3 ప్రశ్నలకు
> సమాధానం ఉంటే చాలు:
>
> 1️⃣ Workshop కి ముందు మీకు ఏ పని ఎక్కువ ఇబ్బంది పెట్టేది?
> 2️⃣ ఇవాళ నేర్చుకున్నదాంట్లో మీకు బాగా ఉపయోగపడేది ఏది?
> 3️⃣ దాని వల్ల ఏం మారుతుందని అనిపిస్తోంది? (సమయం, డబ్బు — ఒక సంఖ్య చెప్తే ఇంకా మంచిది)
>
> తెలుగులోనే రాయండి, ఎలా మాట్లాడతారో అలాగే. సరిచేయాల్సిన అవసరం లేదు 🙏
>
> — సంతోష్

**Why those three questions:** they force the before → action → result structure that makes
a review persuasive. An unprompted "very good sir, thank you" is useless on a landing page.

## Follow-up after 2 days (for non-responders)

> [పేరు] గారు, ఒక్క లైన్ చాలు — workshop లో మీకు బాగా నచ్చింది ఏమిటి? 🙏

## Rules when you paste them in

- **Don't clean up their language.** Telugu-English mixing, small grammar slips, an odd
  turn of phrase — that's exactly what makes a review read as real. Polished reviews read
  as written by the seller.
- **Keep the number** if they gave one (days, hours, rupees). Numbers are what separate a
  review from a compliment.
- **Ask permission** before publishing name + business + town. A WhatsApp "ఇది website లో
  పెట్టవచ్చా?" and a "సరే" is enough — screenshot it and keep it.
- **Full name or first name + initial** is fine. Never invent the town or business.
- **Photos are optional.** If they send one, the card already supports it — put
  `<img src="...">` inside `.tst-av`. If not, the coloured initial looks fine.

## Which three to pick

Pick reviews that each kill a different objection, not the three most flattering:

1. One with a **money or time number** — proves ROI
2. One about the **English / quotation struggle** — the most common pain in this audience
3. One from someone who thought **"I'm not a computer person"** — your single biggest
   drop-off objection. Ask them to mention their age; "నా వయసు 52" does more work than
   any adjective.

## Where else to put them

Once you have 5+, ask the best 2 to leave a **Google review on your business profile**.
Those are verifiable, they help you rank locally, and you can link to the profile from the
page — which is worth more than any on-page quote, because the visitor can check it.
